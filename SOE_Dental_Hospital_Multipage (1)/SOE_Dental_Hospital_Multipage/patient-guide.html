BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Three steps to get started.

01

Send an enquiry

Choose the treatment you want to discuss, share your preferred date and leave a contact number.

02

Confirm appointment details

Ask the clinic to confirm available time slots, consultation charges, location and any preparation needed.

03

Discuss your options

At the visit, explain your symptoms and goals, and ask about findings, alternatives, cost and aftercare.

QUESTIONS PATIENTS OFTEN ASK

Good questions to bring with you.

What should I tell the dentist?

Describe the concern, when it started, what makes it better or worse, any previous dental treatment and any medicines or allergies relevant to your care.

Should I ask for a written estimate?

Yes. Ask for an itemised estimate and clarify whether imaging, temporary work, crowns, follow-up visits and aftercare are included.

Does an online enquiry confirm my appointment?

No. Your request is received for follow-up. The clinic needs to confirm the actual date and time based on availability.

What if I have severe tooth pain or swelling?

Contact a dental professional promptly. If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, rapidly increasing facial or neck swelling, or serious facial injury, seek emergency medical care immediately.

Can I use website information to diagnose myself?

No. Website content is general information. A dental professional needs to assess your situation to recommend suitable care.
